    How it works

    Three steps from danger to help.

    1

    Activate SOS

    Press the button on the bracelet. The ESP32 reads GPS and battery instantly.

    2

    Send alert

    The SIM900A sends an SMS over 2G while live data streams to this dashboard.

    3

    Notify trusted contacts

    Contacts get the location. A responder can press Respond to buzz the bracelet.
